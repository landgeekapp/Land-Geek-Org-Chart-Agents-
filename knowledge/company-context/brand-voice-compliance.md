# Brand Voice & Compliance Guidelines

## Brand Voice

### Tone
- **Authentic**: Real talk, no hype. We've done this, we teach what works.
- **Encouraging**: Supportive without being pushy. Meet people where they are.
- **Expert**: Confident authority from experience, not arrogance.
- **Accessible**: Complex concepts made simple. No jargon walls.

### Voice Characteristics
| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Share real numbers and results | Make vague promises |
| Acknowledge challenges exist | Pretend it's easy money |
| Celebrate student wins specifically | Use generic success language |
| Speak from experience | Lecture or condescend |
| Be direct and actionable | Be wishy-washy or hedge |

### Key Phrases
- "Creating freedom through purpose, joy, and financial peace"
- "Land investing made simple"
- "The path to financial independence"
- "Join a community of action-takers"

### Phrases to Avoid
- "Get rich quick"
- "Passive income" (without context about work required)
- "Guaranteed results"
- "No experience needed" (without proper disclaimers)

## Compliance Requirements

### Earnings Claims (FTC Guidelines)

**NEVER**:
- Guarantee specific income amounts
- Imply typical results without substantiation
- Use outlier results without clear disclaimers

**ALWAYS**:
- Include earnings disclaimer on all marketing with income claims
- Use "results not typical" when sharing success stories
- Substantiate any claims with documented evidence

**Required Disclaimer (adapt as needed)**:
> Results vary. The success stories shared are not typical. Your results will depend on many factors including your effort, skills, and market conditions. We make no guarantees about your income or success.

### Testimonial Consent (FTC + Platform Rules)

**Before using ANY testimonial**:
1. Obtain written consent (email confirmation minimum)
2. Confirm they are a real customer
3. Verify the results claimed are accurate
4. Document consent with date
5. If compensated or incentivized, disclose it

**Consent must include**:
- Permission to use name/likeness
- Permission to use on specified channels
- Acknowledgment results may be edited for length
- Right to revoke (and process to do so)

### Email Compliance (CAN-SPAM)

**Every marketing email must have**:
- Clear sender identification
- Valid physical mailing address
- Obvious unsubscribe mechanism
- Unsubscribes honored within 10 business days

**Subject lines**:
- Must not be deceptive
- Must reflect email content

### SMS/Text Compliance (TCPA)

**Before sending ANY text**:
- Must have explicit written consent
- Must identify sender
- Must provide opt-out mechanism
- Respect quiet hours (typically 8am-9pm recipient's time)

**Consent requirements**:
- Cannot be buried in terms
- Must be separate, clear agreement
- Must specify what they're opting into

### Social Media

**Paid partnerships/Affiliate content**:
- Must disclose with #ad or #sponsored
- Disclosure must be clear and prominent
- Cannot rely on platform's paid partnership tags alone

**Claims in ads**:
- Same rules as other marketing
- Platform-specific restrictions apply
- Document all claims for potential review

## Compliance Checklist for Content

Before publishing any marketing content:

- [ ] No guaranteed income claims
- [ ] Success stories have documented consent
- [ ] Results shown are substantiated
- [ ] Appropriate disclaimers included
- [ ] Email has required elements
- [ ] Disclosures are clear and prominent
- [ ] Content aligns with brand voice
- [ ] No competitor disparagement
- [ ] No false urgency/scarcity
