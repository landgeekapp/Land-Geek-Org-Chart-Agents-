# The Land Geek - Company Overview

## Mission
Creating freedom through purpose, joy, and financial peace.

## Business Model

## Core Values (F.R.E.E.)

### Focus & Flow
Don't sweat the small stuff, stay focused. Prioritize what moves the needle.

### Real Work & Relationships
Do meaningful work with people you respect. Build genuine connections.

### Excellence Always
Bring your best—details create trust. Quality over quantity.

### Evolve Constantly
Kaizen mindset. Keep improving, every day. Embrace change and growth.

## Target Audience
- Aspiring land investors seeking financial freedom
- Real estate professionals diversifying into land
- Entrepreneurs looking for semi-passive income
- Professionals seeking exit from traditional employment

## Value Proposition
- Proven system for land investing success
- Community support and accountability
- Education that leads to real results
- Path to financial independence

## Key Differentiators
- Founder's proven track record
- Comprehensive education + community model
- Focus on student success, not just sales
