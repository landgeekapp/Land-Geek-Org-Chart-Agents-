# SOP: Escalation Procedures

**Owner**: All Departments
**Last Updated**: [Date]
**Version**: 1.0

## Purpose
Ensure issues requiring higher authority are escalated promptly with proper documentation.

## Escalation Matrix

### To CEO

| Trigger | SLA | Required Information |
|---------|-----|---------------------|
| Spending >$500 | 24 hours | Cost breakdown, ROI projection, alternatives |
| Refund request | 4 hours | Client history, reason, retention attempts |
| Formal complaint | 2 hours | Full context, timeline, recommended resolution |
| Legal/compliance | Immediate | All relevant documentation |
| Strategic opportunity | 48 hours | Opportunity brief, resources, risk assessment |
| Hiring request | 1 week | Role justification, budget impact, JD |
| Discount >10% | 24 hours | Deal details, margin impact, recommendation |

### To Head of Operations

| Trigger | SLA | Required Information |
|---------|-----|---------------------|
| System outage | Immediate | Error details, affected users, workarounds |
| Process failure | 4 hours | What failed, impact, root cause if known |
| Data issue | 4 hours | What's wrong, scope, urgency |
| Tool access problem | 24 hours | User, tool, error message |

### To Head of Sales

| Trigger | SLA | Required Information |
|---------|-----|---------------------|
| Upsell opportunity | 48 hours | Client history, current products, recommendation |
| Referral received | 24 hours | Referrer, referral contact, context |

### To Client Success

| Trigger | SLA | Required Information |
|---------|-----|---------------------|
| New client closed | Immediate | Full handoff package per protocol |
| Client complaint (non-formal) | 4 hours | Issue details, client history |

## Escalation Format

When escalating, include:

```
## Escalation: [Brief Title]

**From**: [Your Role]
**To**: [Escalation Target]
**Priority**: [Immediate/High/Standard]
**SLA**: [Response needed by]

### Situation
[What happened / what is being requested]

### Background
[Relevant context and history]

### Impact
[Who/what is affected, urgency level]

### Recommendation
[Your suggested resolution]

### Supporting Data
[Attach relevant documents, screenshots, etc.]
```

## Response Expectations

- **Immediate**: Response within 1 hour
- **High**: Response within 4 hours
- **Standard**: Response within 24 hours

## Tracking
- Log all escalations in [system]
- Track resolution time
- Monthly review of escalation patterns
