# SOP: Lead Qualification

**Owner**: Head of Marketing / Head of Sales
**Last Updated**: [Date]
**Version**: 1.0

## Purpose
Ensure only qualified leads are passed to Sales, maximizing close rates and sales team efficiency.

## Lead Scoring Criteria

### Scoring Matrix (1-10 scale)

| Factor | Weight | Scoring Guide |
|--------|--------|---------------|
| Budget/Investment Capacity | 25% | 1-3: <$5K, 4-6: $5-15K, 7-10: $15K+ |
| Timeline/Urgency | 20% | 1-3: "someday", 4-6: 6+ months, 7-10: <3 months |
| Prior Experience | 15% | 1-3: none, 4-6: real estate adjacent, 7-10: land/RE investor |
| Engagement Level | 20% | 1-3: single touch, 4-6: multiple touches, 7-10: high engagement |
| Goal Clarity | 20% | 1-3: vague, 4-6: general, 7-10: specific and realistic |

### Lead Categories

| Score | Category | Action |
|-------|----------|--------|
| 7.5+ | SQL (Sales Qualified Lead) | Pass to Sales within 2 hours |
| 5.0-7.4 | MQL (Marketing Qualified Lead) | Nurture sequence |
| <5.0 | Unqualified | General newsletter, low-touch |

## Qualification Process

### Marketing Qualification
1. [ ] Track engagement signals:
   - Email opens/clicks
   - Content downloads
   - Webinar attendance
   - Website behavior
2. [ ] Calculate engagement score
3. [ ] Trigger qualification survey/form at threshold
4. [ ] Score responses
5. [ ] Route based on score

### Sales Qualification (for SQLs)
1. [ ] Review Marketing handoff data
2. [ ] Conduct discovery call
3. [ ] Confirm/adjust score
4. [ ] Proceed or return to Marketing for nurture

## Handoff Protocol

**Marketing → Sales** (Score ≥7.5)
- Lead source and channel
- Complete engagement history
- Content consumed
- Qualification score breakdown
- Any notes or signals

**Sales Response SLA**: Contact within 2 hours during business hours

## Metrics
- MQL to SQL conversion: 40%+
- SQL to Opportunity: 60%+
- Lead score accuracy (predicted vs. actual close): 80%+
