### Price/Cost Objections

#### "It's Too Expensive"
**Frequency**: [Track weekly]
**Close Rate When Raised**: [Track weekly]

**Effective Response:**
1. Reframe: "I hear you on price. Before we talk numbers, let's make sure this is the right fit. What would [outcome] be worth to you?"
2. Quantify: "If you could [achieve goal], what would that mean? [Listen] So that's worth [value] to you, right?"
3. Compare: "So if [outcome] is worth [value] and this is [price], that's a [X]X return. The question isn't whether you can afford it—it's whether you can afford NOT to do it."
4. Address: "Is it that you don't see the value, or that you need a different payment structure?"

**Frequency**: [Track weekly]
**Close Rate When Raised**: [Track weekly]

**Effective Response:**

## Weekly Objection Review Meeting

### Agenda (30 minutes, every Monday)

1. **Data Review** (10 min)
   - Top 3 objections from last week
   - Close rates by objection type
   - Trends and patterns

2. **Best Practice Sharing** (10 min)
   - "Who had success with [Objection]? Walk us through it..."
   - Share winning responses
   - Update objection library

3. **Role-Play Practice** (10 min)
   - Practice top objection of the week
   - Get feedback from team
   - Refine responses

### Meeting Output
- [ ] Updated objection response library
- [ ] New scripts/talking points
- [ ] Action items for the week
