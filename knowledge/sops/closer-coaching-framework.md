# SOP: Closer Coaching Framework

**Owner**: Head of Sales
**Last Updated**: [Date]
**Version**: 1.0

## Purpose
Structured framework for coaching underperforming closers back to target performance.

## Coaching Session Structure

### Pre-Session Preparation
- [ ] Review last 2 weeks of performance data
- [ ] Listen to 2-3 call recordings (if available)
- [ ] Review lost deals with notes
- [ ] Compare to top performer metrics
- [ ] Prepare specific examples

### Session Agenda (60-90 minutes)

#### 1. Performance Review (15 min)
**Objective**: Set context, show data

- "Let's look at your numbers from the last 2 weeks..."
- Show comparison: [Closer] vs. [Top Performer]
- "I want to help you get back to [target]%. Let's figure out what's happening."

**Key Metrics to Review:**
- Close rate trend
- Objection frequency
- Average deal size
- Call duration
- Win/loss reasons

#### 2. Deal Review (20 min)
**Objective**: Identify specific issues

- Review 2-3 recent lost deals together
- "Walk me through what happened on [Deal Name]..."
- "What objection did they raise?"
- "How did you respond?"
- "What do you think you could have done differently?"

**Questions to Ask:**
- "What was your read on their interest level?"
- "When did you sense they were pulling back?"
- "What objection handling worked/didn't work?"

#### 3. Role-Play Practice (25 min)
**Objective**: Build skills through practice

**Common Scenarios:**
1. Price objection
2. Competitive comparison
3. "I need to think about it"
4. Timeline concerns
5. Trust/credibility questions

**Role-Play Format:**
- Coach plays prospect, closer practices response
- Switch roles
- Debrief: "What worked? What could be better?"

#### 4. Action Plan (15 min)
**Objective**: Set clear next steps

**Create Specific Actions:**
- [ ] "This week, focus on [specific skill]"
- [ ] "Use this script for [specific objection]"
- [ ] "I'll check in [frequency] to see how it's going"

**Set Expectations:**
- Target close rate: [X]%
- Timeline: [Date]
- Check-in schedule: [Frequency]

#### 5. Resources & Support (5 min)
**Objective**: Provide tools

- Share updated objection scripts
- Share competitive response framework
- Share call recordings from top performers (if available)
- "What else do you need from me to succeed?"

## Coaching Scripts by Objection Type

### Price Objection Handling

**Old Approach (Ineffective):**
- "I understand it's expensive..."
- "Let me see what I can do on price..."
- "What's your budget?"

**New Approach (Effective):**
1. **Reframe**: "I hear you on price. Before we talk numbers, let's make sure this is the right fit. What would [outcome] be worth to you?"
2. **Quantify Value**: "If you could [achieve goal], what would that mean? [Listen] So that's worth [value] to you, right?"
3. **Compare**: "So if [outcome] is worth [value] and this is [price], that's a [X]X return. The question isn't whether you can afford it—it's whether you can afford NOT to do it."
4. **Address**: "Is it that you don't see the value, or that you need a different payment structure?"

### Competitive Objection Handling

**Old Approach (Ineffective):**
- "We're better than them..."
- "They don't have [feature]..."
- "You should choose us because..."

**New Approach (Effective):**
1. **Acknowledge**: "I understand you're looking at [Competitor]. They're a solid option."
2. **Discover**: "What specifically attracted you to them? [Listen] What's most important to you in making this decision?"
3. **Differentiate**: "Based on what you just said, here's why we're the better fit: [specific to their needs]"
4. **Close**: "Given your goal of [their goal], here's why we're the right choice: [value alignment]"

### "I Need to Think About It" Handling

**Old Approach (Ineffective):**
- "Sure, take your time..."
- "When should I follow up?"
- "Let me know if you have questions..."

**New Approach (Effective):**
1. **Uncover Objection**: "Absolutely, I want you to be confident. Help me understand: What specifically do you need to think about?"
2. **Categorize**: "Is it whether this is the right fit, whether you can afford it, or whether now is the right time?"
3. **Address**: "If it's [specific concern], I can help you think through that right now. What's your concern about [specific]?"
4. **Close**: "If we can resolve [concern] right now, is there any reason not to move forward?"

## Post-Coaching Follow-Up

### Daily Check-Ins (Week 1)
- [ ] Morning: "What's your plan for today? Any calls you want to prep for?"
- [ ] End of day: "How did it go? What worked? What didn't?"

### Weekly Review (Week 2+)
- [ ] Review close rate progress
- [ ] Review objection handling improvements
- [ ] Adjust coaching as needed

### Success Metrics
- **Week 1 Target**: Close rate improves by [X] points
- **Week 2 Target**: Close rate at [Y]% (team average)
- **Week 3 Target**: Close rate at [Z]% (above average)

## Escalation Path

If performance doesn't improve after 2 weeks of coaching:
1. **Week 3**: Reduce lead volume temporarily, increase coaching frequency
2. **Week 4**: Performance improvement plan with specific metrics
3. **Week 6**: Escalate to CEO for decision on continued employment
