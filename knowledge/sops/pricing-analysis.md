## Value Ladder Approach

### Framework: Show ROI Before Price

**Step 1: Establish Value**
- "Before we talk price, let's make sure this is the right fit. What would [outcome] be worth to you?"
- "If you could [achieve goal] in [timeframe], what would that mean for you?"

**Step 2: Quantify the Outcome**
- "Based on what you've told me, here's what success looks like: [specific outcome]"
- "Our students typically see [result] within [timeframe]"

**Step 3: Compare Investment to Return**
- "So if [outcome] is worth [value] to you, and this investment is [price], that's a [X]X return, right?"
- "The question isn't whether you can afford it—it's whether you can afford NOT to do it."

**Step 4: Address Price Objection**
- "I hear you on price. Let me ask: Is it that you don't see the value, or that you need a different payment structure?"
- "What would need to be true for this to be a no-brainer investment?"

## Pricing Response Scripts

### Script 1: "It's Too Expensive"
**Response:**
"I understand price is a consideration. Let me ask: Compared to what? 

If you're comparing this to [doing nothing/staying where you are], what's that costing you? [Their current situation cost]

If you're comparing this to [competitor], here's the difference: [value differentiation]

The real question is: What's the cost of not getting [outcome]? Because that's what you're really investing in—not a course, but [their transformation]."

### Script 2: "I Need to Think About It" (Price-Related)
**Response:**
"Absolutely, I want you to be confident in your decision. Help me understand: What specifically do you need to think about?

Is it:
- Whether this is the right fit? [Address fit]
- Whether you can afford it? [Address payment options]
- Whether now is the right time? [Address urgency]

Because if it's [specific concern], I can help you think through that right now."

### Script 3: "Can You Do Better on Price?"
**Response:**
"I appreciate you asking. Here's what I can do within my authority: [10% discount or payment plan].

But let me be honest: The value isn't in the discount—it's in [specific outcomes]. A 10% discount saves you $[amount], but if you don't get [outcome], you've lost $[much larger amount].

What I can do is [discount/payment plan] + [value-add], which actually gives you more value than just a bigger discount. Does that work?"
