## Competitive Objection Tracking

### When Prospect Mentions Competitor

**Questions to Ask:**
1. "What specifically attracted you to [Competitor]?"
2. "What's your timeline for making a decision?"
3. "What would need to be true for us to be the right choice?"

**Response Framework:**
1. **Acknowledge** - "I understand you're looking at [Competitor]. They're a solid option."
2. **Differentiate** - "Here's what makes us different..."
3. **Value** - "The key difference is [specific value]..."
4. **Close** - "Given [their goal], here's why we're the better fit..."

## Competitive Response Scripts

### Script 1: Price Comparison Objection
**When:** "I found [Competitor] for $[Lower Price]"

**Response:**
"I appreciate you doing your research. The difference in price reflects the difference in value. Here's what you get with us that you won't get there:

1. [Specific value point 1]
2. [Specific value point 2]
3. [Specific value point 3]

The real question isn't the price difference—it's whether you want [outcome] or [lesser outcome]. Based on your goal of [their goal], which path gets you there faster?"

### Script 2: Feature Comparison Objection
**When:** "They offer [Feature] that you don't"

**Response:**
"That's a fair point. Let me ask: Is [Feature] what you actually need to achieve [their goal], or is it what sounds good? 

What we've found is that [our approach] actually gets better results because [reason]. Here's what our students say about [our approach] vs. [their feature]...

The question is: Do you want [their feature] or do you want [outcome]?"

### Script 3: "Considering Alternatives" Objection
**When:** "I'm looking at a few options"

**Response:**
"Smart move to compare. Let me help you make the right decision. 

What are the top 3 things you need to see results? [Listen]

Based on that, here's how we stack up:
1. [Need 1] - We deliver this by [method]
2. [Need 2] - We deliver this by [method]
3. [Need 3] - We deliver this by [method]

What questions do you have about how we deliver on those?"
