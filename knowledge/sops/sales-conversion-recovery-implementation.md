## Escalation Path

**If conversion doesn't improve after 2 weeks:**

1. **Week 3 Actions:**
   - Reduce Jon's lead volume temporarily
   - Increase coaching frequency to daily
   - Consider pairing Jon with top performer for shadowing

2. **Week 4 Actions:**
   - Formal performance improvement plan
   - Specific metrics and timeline
   - Escalate to CEO for review

3. **Week 6 Decision Point:**
   - CEO decision on continued employment
   - Consider reassignment or termination
