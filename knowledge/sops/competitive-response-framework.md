# Competitive Response Framework

**Owner**: Head of Sales
**Last Updated**: [Date]
**Version**: 1.0

## Purpose
Provide sales team with structured responses to competitive objections, ensuring consistent messaging and higher close rates.

## Framework Structure

### Step 1: Acknowledge (Don't Dismiss)
**Why**: Prospects have done research. Dismissing competitors makes you look defensive.

**Script:**
"I understand you're looking at [Competitor]. They're a solid option, and I appreciate you doing your research."

### Step 2: Discover (Understand Their Why)
**Why**: You can't differentiate effectively without knowing what they value.

**Questions to Ask:**
- "What specifically attracted you to [Competitor]?"
- "What's most important to you in making this decision?"
- "What would success look like for you?"
- "What concerns do you have about [Competitor]?"

### Step 3: Differentiate (Show Your Advantage)
**Why**: Based on what they value, show why you're the better fit.

**Script Structure:**
"Based on what you just told me about [their priority], here's why we're the better fit:
- [Advantage 1 - tied to their priority]
- [Advantage 2 - tied to their priority]
- [Advantage 3 - tied to their priority]"

### Step 4: Close (Tie to Their Goal)
**Why**: Bring it back to their desired outcome.

**Script:**
"Given your goal of [their goal], here's why we're the right choice: [value alignment]. What questions do you have about how we deliver on that?"

## Price Comparison Responses

### When Competitor is Cheaper

**Objection:**
"[Competitor] is $[Lower Amount], you're $[Higher Amount]"

**Response:**
"I appreciate you doing your research. The difference in price reflects the difference in value.

Here's what you get with us that you won't get there:
1. [Specific value point 1 - quantifiable]
2. [Specific value point 2 - quantifiable]
3. [Specific value point 3 - quantifiable]

The real question isn't the $[Difference] price difference—it's whether you want [outcome] or [lesser outcome]. 

Based on your goal of [their goal], which investment gets you there faster and more reliably? Because that $[Difference] difference is actually [X]% of your first deal, which you'll get [timeframe] faster with us."

### When Competitor is More Expensive

**Objection:**
"[Competitor] is $[Higher Amount], you're $[Lower Amount]"

**Response:**
"I understand you're comparing options. Here's the thing: Price isn't the only factor—it's value.

For $[Our Amount], you get:
- [Value point 1]
- [Value point 2]
- [Value point 3]

[Competitor] charges $[Higher Amount] for [what they offer]. The question is: Do you need [their extra features] or do you need [outcome]?

Our students get [outcome] without paying the premium. What matters more to you: [their extra features] or [outcome]?"

## Generic Competitive Responses

### "I'm Considering a Few Options"

**Response:**
"Smart move to compare. Let me help you make the right decision.

What are the top 3 things you need to see results? [Listen - write them down]

Based on that, here's how we stack up:
1. [Need 1] - We deliver this by [method] → [Result]
2. [Need 2] - We deliver this by [method] → [Result]
3. [Need 3] - We deliver this by [method] → [Result]

What questions do you have about how we deliver on those?"

### "They Offer [Feature] That You Don't"

**Response:**
"That's a fair point. Let me ask: Is [Feature] what you actually need to achieve [their goal], or is it what sounds good?

What we've found is that [our approach] actually gets better results because [reason]. Here's what our students say about [our approach] vs. [their feature]:

[Testimonial or case study]

The question is: Do you want [their feature] or do you want [outcome]? Because [outcome] is what you're really investing in."

## Objection Prevention

### Proactive Competitive Positioning

**Early in the Call:**
"I'm sure you're looking at a few options. That's smart. Before we dive in, what are the top 3 things you need to see results? [Listen]

Great. As we go through this, I'll show you exactly how we deliver on those. That way, you can compare apples to apples when you're making your decision."

**This:**
- Sets expectations
- Positions you as consultative
- Makes comparison easier later
- Reduces competitive objections
