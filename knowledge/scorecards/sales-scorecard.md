# Head of Sales Scorecard

## Role Summary
**Mission**: Drive revenue growth through effective lead conversion while delivering genuine value.

## Key Performance Indicators

### Revenue
| Metric | Target | Frequency |
|--------|--------|-----------|
| Monthly revenue | 100%+ of quota | Monthly |
| Revenue vs quota | 100%+ | Monthly |
| Average deal size | Maintain/increase | Monthly |
| Upsell revenue | 20% of total | Quarterly |

### Conversion
| Metric | Target | Frequency |
|--------|--------|-----------|
| Lead-to-close rate | 15%+ | Weekly |
| SQL-to-close rate | 25%+ | Weekly |
| Sales cycle length | ≤14 days | Monthly |
| Demo-to-close rate | 40%+ | Weekly |

### Pipeline Health
| Metric | Target | Frequency |
|--------|--------|-----------|
| Pipeline coverage | 3x monthly target | Weekly |
| Pipeline velocity | Increasing trend | Monthly |
| Forecast accuracy | 85%+ | Monthly |
| Qualified opportunities | 20+ per week | Weekly |

### Efficiency
| Metric | Target | Frequency |
|--------|--------|-----------|
| Customer acquisition cost | Within budget | Monthly |
| Time to first contact | <2 hours | Daily |
| CRM data completeness | 95%+ | Weekly |
| Activities per rep | 50+ per day | Daily |

## Scoring
- Green: Meeting or exceeding target
- Yellow: Within 10% of target
- Red: More than 10% below target
