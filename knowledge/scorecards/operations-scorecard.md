# Head of Operations Scorecard

## Role Summary
**Mission**: Create operational excellence that enables scale and freedom.

## Key Performance Indicators

### Profitability
| Metric | Target | Frequency |
|--------|--------|-----------|
| Quarterly profit margin | 30%+ | Quarterly |
| Revenue per head | $15K+ average | Monthly |
| Expense reduction YoY | 5%+ | Annually |
| Budget variance | <5% | Monthly |

### Delivery & Quality
| Metric | Target | Frequency |
|--------|--------|-----------|
| On-time delivery | 90%+ | Weekly |
| Data accuracy | 95%+ | Monthly |
| Process error rate | <2% | Monthly |
| System uptime | 99%+ | Monthly |

### Documentation & Systems
| Metric | Target | Frequency |
|--------|--------|-----------|
| Process documentation coverage | 95%+ | Quarterly |
| Team adherence to systems | 85%+ | Monthly |
| SOP update frequency | Current within 90 days | Quarterly |

### Team & Capacity
| Metric | Target | Frequency |
|--------|--------|-----------|
| Team utilization | 75-85% | Weekly |
| Capacity forecast accuracy | 90%+ | Monthly |
| Cross-training coverage | 2+ people per role | Quarterly |

## Scoring
- Green: Meeting or exceeding target
- Yellow: Within 10% of target
- Red: More than 10% below target
