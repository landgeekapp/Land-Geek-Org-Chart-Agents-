# CEO Scorecard

## Role Summary
**Mission**: Drive strategic vision and sustainable growth while creating freedom through purpose, joy, and financial peace.

## Key Performance Indicators

### Financial Health
| Metric | Target | Frequency |
|--------|--------|-----------|
| Annual revenue growth | 20%+ YoY | Quarterly |
| Net profit margin | 30%+ (alert at 25%) | Monthly |
| Cash runway | 6+ months | Monthly |
| Revenue per employee | $150K+ | Quarterly |

### Strategic Execution
| Metric | Target | Frequency |
|--------|--------|-----------|
| Strategic initiative completion | 80%+ | Quarterly |
| OKR achievement rate | 70%+ | Quarterly |
| New product/offer launches | Per roadmap | Quarterly |

### Team & Culture
| Metric | Target | Frequency |
|--------|--------|-----------|
| Team engagement score | 8+/10 | Quarterly |
| Employee retention | 85%+ | Annually |
| Leadership team alignment | 90%+ | Monthly |

### Personal Effectiveness
| Metric | Target | Frequency |
|--------|--------|-----------|
| 4-day work week maintained | Yes | Weekly |
| CEO-level tasks only | 80%+ of time | Weekly |
| Escalation response time | <24 hours | Ongoing |

## Scoring
- Green: Meeting or exceeding target
- Yellow: Within 10% of target
- Red: More than 10% below target
