# Head of Marketing Scorecard

## Role Summary
**Mission**: Attract qualified prospects, build brand authority, and deliver high-quality leads to sales.

## Key Performance Indicators

### Lead Generation
| Metric | Target | Frequency |
|--------|--------|-----------|
| Marketing qualified leads (MQLs) | [Define target] | Weekly |
| Cost per lead (CPL) | Within budget | Weekly |
| Lead quality score | 7+/10 average | Weekly |
| MQL to SQL conversion | 40%+ | Monthly |

### Content & Engagement
| Metric | Target | Frequency |
|--------|--------|-----------|
| Content engagement rate | 5%+ | Weekly |
| Email open rate | 25%+ | Weekly |
| Email click rate | 3%+ | Weekly |
| Social engagement rate | 3%+ | Weekly |

### Growth
| Metric | Target | Frequency |
|--------|--------|-----------|
| Email list growth | 10%+ | Monthly |
| Organic traffic growth | 15%+ | Quarterly |
| Social followers growth | 5%+ | Monthly |
| SEO keyword rankings | Top 10 for targets | Monthly |

### Efficiency
| Metric | Target | Frequency |
|--------|--------|-----------|
| Marketing ROI | 3x+ | Monthly |
| Campaign ROI | 2x+ minimum | Per campaign |
| Budget utilization | 95-100% | Monthly |
| Content production cadence | Per calendar | Weekly |

## Scoring
- Green: Meeting or exceeding target
- Yellow: Within 10% of target
- Red: More than 10% below target
