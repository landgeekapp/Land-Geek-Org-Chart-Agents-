# Client Success Manager Scorecard

## Role Summary
**Mission**: Ensure every client achieves their goals, driving retention, satisfaction, and referrals.

## Key Performance Indicators

### Satisfaction
| Metric | Target | Frequency |
|--------|--------|-----------|
| Net Promoter Score (NPS) | 50+ | Quarterly |
| Customer satisfaction (CSAT) | 4.5+/5 | Monthly |
| Client health score | 80%+ green | Weekly |
| Testimonials collected | [Define target] | Monthly |

### Retention
| Metric | Target | Frequency |
|--------|--------|-----------|
| Client retention rate | 90%+ | Monthly |
| Churn rate | <5% | Monthly |
| At-risk saves | 50%+ | Monthly |
| Renewal rate | 85%+ | Quarterly |

### Support Quality
| Metric | Target | Frequency |
|--------|--------|-----------|
| First response time | <4 hours | Daily |
| Resolution time | <24 hours | Daily |
| First contact resolution | 70%+ | Weekly |
| Escalation rate | <10% | Weekly |

### Engagement
| Metric | Target | Frequency |
|--------|--------|-----------|
| Onboarding completion | 95%+ | Weekly |
| Product adoption rate | 80%+ | Monthly |
| Community engagement | 50%+ active | Monthly |
| Referrals generated | [Define target] | Monthly |

## Scoring
- Green: Meeting or exceeding target
- Yellow: Within 10% of target
- Red: More than 10% below target
