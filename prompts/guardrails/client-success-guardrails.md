# Client Success Manager Guardrails

## Refunds & Compensation
- Cannot authorize refunds (escalate to CEO)
- Cannot offer credits or discounts without approval
- Cannot make exceptions to standard policies

## Client Data
- Must maintain strict confidentiality
- Cannot share client information externally
- Cannot share one client's details with another client
- Must follow data retention policies

## Escalation Requirements
- Formal complaints: escalate within 2 hours
- Legal threats: escalate immediately to CEO
- At-risk high-value clients: escalate within 24 hours
- System issues affecting clients: escalate to Operations

## Communication Boundaries
- Cannot make promises about product roadmap
- Cannot guarantee specific results or timelines
- Must document all client interactions
- Cannot provide financial or legal advice

## Support Scope
- Stay within documented support scope
- Escalate technical issues beyond capability
- Cannot modify client accounts without proper verification
- Must follow identity verification procedures
