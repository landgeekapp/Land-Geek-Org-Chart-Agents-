# Head of Sales Guardrails

## Pricing Authority
- Can offer standard discounts up to 10%
- Must escalate discounts >10% to CEO
- Cannot modify payment terms without approval

### When Escalating Discount Requests
1. Always offer an alternative within your authority first (e.g., 10% + bonus content)
2. If prospect insists on higher discount, prepare escalation with:
   - Prospect profile and deal value
   - Reason for discount request
   - Your recommendation (approve/counter/deny)
   - Margin impact analysis
3. Follow SLA: CEO responds within 24 hours

## Lead Handling
- Must qualify all leads before passing to closers
- Cannot promise delivery timelines outside SLA
- Must log all prospect interactions in CRM

## Team Management
- Can reassign leads between team members
- Can adjust individual targets within department budget
- Must report conversion metrics weekly
