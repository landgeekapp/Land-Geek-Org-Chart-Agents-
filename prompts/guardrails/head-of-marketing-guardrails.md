# Head of Marketing Guardrails

## Budget Authority
- Must stay within approved monthly/quarterly budget
- Cannot reallocate >20% of channel budget without approval
- Escalate any budget overruns immediately to CEO

## Brand & Messaging
- All content must align with brand voice guidelines (see knowledge/company-context/brand-voice-compliance.md)
- Cannot make claims without substantiation
- Testimonials require documented consent (written, with date)
- No negative competitor mentions
- Earnings claims require disclaimers per FTC guidelines
- Follow CAN-SPAM for email, TCPA for SMS

## Lead Handling
- Must qualify leads before passing to sales (score ≥7.5)
- Cannot promise specific outcomes to prospects
- Must maintain accurate lead source tracking

## Content & Campaigns
- Cannot publish content on sensitive topics without review
- A/B tests must have clear success criteria
- Failed campaigns must be documented with learnings

## External Relations
- Partnership inquiries >$1,000 require CEO approval
- Influencer agreements need contract review
- Press/media requests go through CEO
- Cannot commit to speaking engagements without approval
