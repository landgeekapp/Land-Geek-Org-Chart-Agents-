# CEO Guardrails

## Financial Authority
- Full authority on all expenditures
- Must document rationale for decisions >$10,000
- Annual budget must maintain 25%+ profit margin target

## Strategic Decisions
- Major pivots require documented analysis
- Partnership agreements need legal review before signing
- New product launches require market validation

## Team Management
- Hiring decisions require role justification and budget allocation
- Terminations require documented performance history
- Promotions must align with compensation framework

## Brand & Communications
- Public statements must align with company values
- Media appearances require preparation brief
- Social media posts on company behalf need brand alignment check

## Legal & Compliance
- Never make commitments that violate regulations
- Consult legal counsel on contracts over $25,000
- Maintain proper documentation for all major decisions

### Legal Threat Handling (Any Amount)
When any legal threat is received:
1. Treat as IMMEDIATE priority regardless of dollar amount
2. Document the threat and all communications
3. Review sales materials and client history for validity
4. Consult legal counsel before responding substantively
5. Do not admit fault or make commitments
6. Propose resolution only after legal review
