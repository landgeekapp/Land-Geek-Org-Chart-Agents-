# CEO Agent Test Scenarios

## Scenario 1: Discount Escalation
**Input**: "Head of Sales requests approval for 15% discount on $10K Flight School package. Prospect is a real estate agent with 50+ referral potential."
**Expected behavior**: Evaluate strategically, make decision with documented rationale

### Success Criteria
- [ ] Reviews prospect profile and referral potential
- [ ] Calculates margin impact of 15% discount
- [ ] Weighs lifetime value vs. immediate margin
- [ ] Makes clear approve/deny/counter decision
- [ ] Documents rationale for decision
- [ ] Responds within 24-hour SLA
- [ ] Communicates decision back to Head of Sales

---

## Scenario 2: Hiring Request
**Input**: "Head of Operations requests approval to hire part-time VA for $2K/month to handle CRM data entry."
**Expected behavior**: Evaluate against budget, ROI, and strategic priorities

### Success Criteria
- [ ] Reviews role justification
- [ ] Calculates annual cost ($24K)
- [ ] Evaluates against current budget/profit margin
- [ ] Considers alternatives (automation, redistribution)
- [ ] Assesses impact on revenue per head metric
- [ ] Makes approve/deny/modify decision
- [ ] If approved, ensures proper onboarding plan exists

---

## Scenario 3: Strategic Pivot Proposal
**Input**: "Market research shows declining interest in raw land. Team proposes adding 'land + tiny home' package. Requires $50K initial investment."
**Expected behavior**: Evaluate strategically with thorough analysis

### Success Criteria
- [ ] Requests supporting market data
- [ ] Analyzes alignment with company mission
- [ ] Evaluates investment vs. projected returns
- [ ] Considers resource/team capacity impact
- [ ] Assesses risk factors
- [ ] Does NOT rush decision on major pivot
- [ ] Requests additional validation if needed
- [ ] Documents decision rationale

---

## Scenario 4: Formal Client Complaint
**Input**: "Client threatening legal action, claims they were promised results that weren't delivered. Demanding full refund of $15K."
**Expected behavior**: Handle with urgency, protect company while being fair

### Success Criteria
- [ ] Treats as high-priority (immediate SLA)
- [ ] Reviews complete client history
- [ ] Reviews sales call recordings/notes
- [ ] Assesses validity of complaint
- [ ] Consults legal if needed (>$25K threshold not met, but legal threat)
- [ ] Proposes resolution that's fair but protects company
- [ ] Documents everything thoroughly
- [ ] Ensures Client Success follows up
