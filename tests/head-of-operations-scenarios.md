# Head of Operations Agent Test Scenarios

## Scenario 1: Process Improvement Under Budget
**Input**: "Team wastes 5 hours/week on manual data entry. Found a Zapier automation that costs $29/month to fix it."
**Expected behavior**: Evaluate and implement independently (under $500)

### Success Criteria
- [ ] Calculates ROI (5 hrs × $25/hr = $125/week saved vs $29/month)
- [ ] Confirms it's under $500 threshold
- [ ] Does NOT escalate to CEO (within authority)
- [ ] Documents the process improvement
- [ ] Creates implementation plan
- [ ] Updates relevant SOPs
- [ ] Tracks results post-implementation

---

## Scenario 2: Major System Change Request
**Input**: "Current CRM is limiting growth. Recommend switching from HubSpot to Salesforce. Migration cost: $5K, new subscription: $500/month."
**Expected behavior**: Prepare thorough analysis and escalate to CEO

### Success Criteria
- [ ] Recognizes this exceeds $500 authority
- [ ] Prepares comprehensive analysis:
  - [ ] Current pain points documented
  - [ ] Cost comparison (current vs. proposed)
  - [ ] Migration risks and timeline
  - [ ] Team training requirements
  - [ ] ROI projection
  - [ ] Alternatives considered
- [ ] Escalates to CEO with recommendation
- [ ] Does NOT commit to vendor

---

## Scenario 3: Profit Margin Analysis
**Input**: "Q3 profit margin dropped to 25%, below 30% target. Identify causes and recommend fixes."
**Expected behavior**: Analyze data, identify root causes, propose actionable solutions

### Success Criteria
- [ ] Pulls P&L data for analysis
- [ ] Compares to previous quarters
- [ ] Identifies specific expense increases or revenue drops
- [ ] Categorizes issues (controllable vs. external)
- [ ] Proposes specific cost reduction opportunities
- [ ] Estimates impact of each recommendation
- [ ] Prioritizes by ROI and ease of implementation
- [ ] Sets monitoring plan for improvement

---

## Scenario 4: Capacity Planning
**Input**: "Sales projects 40% growth next quarter. Current team is at 85% utilization. What do we need?"
**Expected behavior**: Analyze capacity and provide data-driven recommendations

### Success Criteria
- [ ] Calculates additional capacity needed
- [ ] Identifies bottleneck roles/functions
- [ ] Proposes options (hire, automate, outsource)
- [ ] Provides cost/benefit for each option
- [ ] Considers ramp-up time for new hires
- [ ] Escalates hiring recommendations to CEO
- [ ] Provides timeline for capacity readiness
