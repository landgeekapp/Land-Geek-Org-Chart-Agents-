# Agent Communication Protocol

## Sales → Client Success Handoff
When: New client closes
Data passed: Client profile, purchase details, stated goals, notes from sales calls
Client Success action: Initiate onboarding within 24 hours

## Marketing → Sales Handoff
When: MQL becomes SQL (lead quality score ≥7.5)
Data passed: Lead source, engagement history, content consumed, qualification score
Sales action: Contact within 2 hours during business hours

## Client Success → Sales
When: Upsell opportunity identified or referral received
Data passed: Client history, current products, recommended upgrade, referral contact info
Sales action:
- Upsells: Review and reach out within 48 hours
- Referrals: Contact within 24 hours with personalized approach

## Client Success → Marketing
When: Testimonial opportunity, case study candidate, or product feedback
Data passed: Client story, results achieved, consent status, feedback details
Marketing action:
- Testimonials: Schedule capture within 1 week
- Case studies: Initiate interview process
- Feedback: Log and include in monthly product review

## All Departments → CEO (Escalation Paths)
| Trigger | SLA | Data Required |
|---------|-----|---------------|
| Spending >$500 | 24 hours | Cost breakdown, ROI projection, alternatives considered |
| Refund request | 4 hours | Client history, reason, retention attempts made |
| Formal complaint | 2 hours | Full context, timeline, recommended resolution |
| Legal/compliance concern | Immediate | All relevant documentation |
| Strategic opportunity | 48 hours | Opportunity brief, resource requirements, risk assessment |
| Hiring request | 1 week | Role justification, budget impact, job description |

## Operations → All Departments
Weekly: Dashboard updates, KPI reports
Monthly: Process improvement recommendations
Quarterly: Budget reviews, capacity planning

## CEO → All Departments
As needed: Strategic directives, policy changes, company announcements
Quarterly: Vision updates, goal setting, performance reviews
